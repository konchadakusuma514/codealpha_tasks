Feature Engineering & Pipeline

The model predicts base compensation using multi-dimensional categorical encodings and continuous work-model parameters:

  • 1. Job Role Encoding: Standardized NLP categorization Categorical
  • 2. Seniority Multiplier: EN (0-2y), MI (2-5y), SE (5-8y), EX (8y+) Ordinal
  • 3. Location / Country: Global economic market indexing Categorical
  • 4. Remote Work Ratio: Continuous scale (0% on-site to 100% WFH) Numeric
  • 5. Company Scale: Small (<50), Medium (50-250), Large (250+) Categorical
Model Governance & Reliability
Confidence Interval Calculation

Predictions are bounded by $\pm \text{MAE}$ calculated against unseen validation partitions to ensure transparent risk modeling.

Ensemble Fallbacks

When encountering unseen locations or roles, the engine utilizes Bayesian backoff mechanisms to interpolate from regional averages.
